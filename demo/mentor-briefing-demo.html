Four rules this screen obeys

The posture is the biggest thingNot the metrics. The largest text is what a wise friend would say — the data is small, and deliberately so.
The evidence sits beside it"She has an unanswered request." The mentor can reject the reasoning, not merely the conclusion. A suggestion that hides its basis is a command.
Refusing is as easy as accepting"Doesn't fit her" is the same size as "I used this." A suggestion you cannot decline is an instruction — and instructing a mentor is exactly what we refuse to build.
No scripts. Ever.There is no "send this message" button. No drafted text. The friar speaks in his own voice or the tool has failed.

And there is no member-facing version of this screen. Emma never receives doctrine from a machine. The system briefs the human who accompanies her — and then gets out of the way.

Try it: dismiss both suggestions. The screen does not argue, does not re-rank, does not try again. It simply says: go be with her.
